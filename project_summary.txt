 ■■ InvestorShield
 AI-Assisted Financial Scam & Misinformation Risk Analyzer
 
 Final Project Concept & MVP Blueprint
 
Core message: “Before you trust it, check it.”
InvestorShield helps retail investors identify potential financial scams and misleading investment content before they
act by analyzing suspicious messages, claims, and URLs, explaining risk indicators, and guiding users toward safer
verification steps.

1. The Problem
Investors increasingly encounter financial content through WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, YouTube, SMS,
websites, investment advertisements, trading groups, and online communities. A first-time investor may not know
which claims require verification, whether a regulatory authority is genuinely involved, whether a return promise is a
warning sign, whether a URL has suspicious characteristics, or what to do before transferring money.
Example suspicious message:
“SEBI approved investment opportunity. Guaranteed 30% returns every month. Join our
Telegram group now.”
2. Our Solution
InvestorShield acts as a pre-action safety layer. The user does not need to understand cybersecurity or financial
regulations. They simply provide a message, URL, or financial claim. InvestorShield analyzes the input and produces
an explainable risk assessment.
Core principle
Do not simply say “Scam.” Detect → Explain → Verify → Pause → Act safely.

4. What InvestorShield Detects
A. Financial-return signals

 Guaranteed returns
 Fixed returns

 Unrealistic profit claims
Example: “Guaranteed 30% returns every month.” The system identifies guaranteed-return language as a
high-severity risk indicator.
B. Pressure & urgency
 Act now
 Join now
 Limited time
 Last chance
 Don't miss
 Invest today
Urgency is treated as a risk indicator rather than proof of fraud.
C. Regulatory authority claims
 SEBI approved
 SEBI authorized
 SEBI registered
 Government approved
The system presents these as claims that require verification rather than automatically declaring them false.
D. URL risk signals
 HTTP instead of HTTPS
 IP address used as hostname
 Sensitive or promotional URL keywords
 Excessive subdomains
 Unusually long URLs
 Unusual URL characters
The current URL analyzer examines the URL string locally and does not visit the supplied URL.

8. Safe-Action Guidance
 Do not transfer money until important claims are independently verified.
 Do not share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or other sensitive credentials.
 Verify the organization or person using an official source.
 Be cautious when the message creates urgency or pressure to act immediately.
InvestorShield should guide the user toward safer verification rather than telling the user which investment to choose.

Phase 3 — ML classification

The system should not turn an ML prediction into a definitive declaration of fraud. It should communicate uncertainty
and explain the evidence.
10. Regional-Language Support
A major future enhancement is support for regional-language users, initially English and Tamil. The goal is to detect
the same risk patterns in regional-language financial messages and explain the warnings in the user's preferred
language.
11. Target Users
 First-time retail investors
 Tier-2/3 users
 Regional-language users
 Senior citizens
 People receiving investment messages on WhatsApp or Telegram
 Users exposed to social-media investment promotions
 People about to act on an unfamiliar investment opportunity
12. Privacy-by-Design
InvestorShield should not request or process unnecessary sensitive credentials.
 OTP
 Password
 UPI PIN
 Bank credentials
 PAN
 Aadhaar
 Trading-account credentials
 Private financial records
The user only needs to provide content they want analyzed: a message, URL, or claim.

17. Final Project Positioning
 InvestorShield
 An AI-assisted pre-action safety layer for retail investors that detects and explains
potential scam and misinformation indicators in financial messages, claims and URLs,
helping users pause, verify and make safer decisions.
The project is designed around investor resilience rather than investment advice. Its purpose is to help users
recognize warning signs, understand uncertainty, verify important claims, and avoid acting under pressure.
